2026 Goals

January 18, 2026 | 2 minutes | Notes

5% of 2026 is already behind us. Many of us are still planning this year’s goals. I’ve deliberately trimmed my goals for this year and limited them to three broad areas. This year, I’ll not list all my elaborate details in this post. Instead, I’ll share an update at the end of this year, assuming I manage to follow through.

By then, I aim to have built and deployed multiple apps using Claude Code. Some apps that I built last year are already running locally on my phone and I use them everyday. I’ll publish them soon on the App Store. Last year, I also published a comic book on Amazon Kindle and will share a separate blog on how to do it over the weekend.

This year, my focus areas will primarily be these. Not looking beyond 2026 for now.

  • Fitness: Setting up a home gym
  • Play football once a week and start playing tennis
  • Continuing to improve on my trading skills, especially for crypto and commodities
  • Shipping more products
  • Learning a new musical instrument and creating music using AI
  • Reading more than I have over the past two years
  • Expanding and revising this website with significantly more depth. I don’t enjoy consuming most content on LinkedIn or Instagram and will try to replicate my personal related content here
  • Travel atleast twice

The last couple of years have been deeply transformative for me, especially in helping me understand overlooked areas such as mental health and human behaviour. I thought I knew basics of psychology, until I connected the dots and read a LOT on it last year. For this year, I want to stay focused on the goals I’ve set for myself and keep executing, one at a time.
